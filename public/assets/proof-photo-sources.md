# Before/after demo photos

Created with the built-in image generation tool on 27 October 2026 for scene 6 of KordifyProofReel. These are illustrative dummy photographs, not real customer evidence. Original generated PNGs were copied into this project, retaining the original outputs.

- Before: `public/assets/proof-before-demo.png`
- After: `public/assets/proof-after-demo.png`

## Before prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: generic BEFORE demo photograph for a cleaning-service before/after pair in a product reel. Create one landscape 3:2 realistic photograph, close three-quarter overhead view of a simple white ceramic rectangular washbasin with one chrome faucet on a pale beige tiled countertop, neutral cream tile wall behind. The basin and surrounding counter are visibly dirty: obvious brown soap scum, dull water marks and dust around the rim, a few small smudges, showing it needs cleaning. Ordinary believable grime, not extreme decay. Center the sink and faucet; make the dirty versus clean state very readable at small thumbnail size, wide framing that also survives a central 2.3:1 crop. Natural soft daylight, neutral warm tones, realistic textures and simple composition. No people, hands, cleaning equipment, personal objects, brands, readable text, addresses, watermark, labels, borders, or collage. This will be an illustrative dummy photo, not a real customer job.

## After prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Input image: edit target, the BEFORE dummy photo of a dirty washbasin. Create the matching AFTER photograph of this exact same washbasin after thorough professional cleaning. Remove all brown soap scum, dust, smudges and water marks from the ceramic basin, rim, faucet and beige countertop. White ceramic is clean and bright, chrome faucet is polished, beige tiles are clean. Preserve exactly the same sink shape, drain and overflow placement, faucet design and position, tile joints, countertop, camera angle, framing, lighting and image dimensions. The only change is cleanliness. Photorealistic ordinary cleaning result, no artificial sparkle symbols. No new objects, people, tools, text, logos, labels, watermark, collage or borders.

The before photograph was used as the edit target for the after photograph.
