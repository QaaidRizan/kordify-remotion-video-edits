  https://play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges/static/images/badges/en_badge_web_generic.png

- `apple-app-store-icon.png`: App Store icon from Apple's App Store overview.
  https://www.apple.com/v/app-store/c/images/overview/icon_appstore__ev0z770zyxoy_large_2x.png
  Source page: https://www.apple.com/app-store/
  Badge guidance: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/

The iPhone panel pairs the App Store icon with explicit “iPhone coming soon” copy.
It does not use a Download or Pre-order badge, because neither availability is
claimed by the approved script. Both store assets remain upright in scene 11.
The original files are retained without recoloring or changing their contents.
